# Code of conduct

Eleet is a project about language, history and curiosity, and it welcomes contributors of every
background, language and level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in their second or third language.
- Credit the work of others, and say so when you are unsure.
- Keep discussions focused and constructive; disagree with evidence.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Using the project's tools or spaces to plan harm against people or systems.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation in response to behaviour that
violates this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's contact
options; reports are handled confidentially.
